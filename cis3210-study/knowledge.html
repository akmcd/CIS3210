Where these come from

Source. Each item matches one question from the Kurose & Ross online Knowledge Checks, as transcribed in daf2a/Computer_Networking_Problemset (December 2023). That repo's README says 7th edition, but the question set follows the online site's 8th-edition layout (for example, Socket Programming is 2.7 there). Section numbers on this page follow your 9th edition.

Wording. The setups, numbers and correct answers follow the originals; the question text is reworded, because the original wording belongs to the authors. Every answer was checked against the 9th edition, and an answer-key note appears wherever the transcription's key disagrees with it.

What each tag means.

  • Original choices The original's choices (true/false, matching pairs, or a list in the question) and its answer are both available.
  • Shared choice list Sibling questions in the original shared one list; the choices here are their combined answers.
  • Choices added The original was a calculation or fill-in; its setup and answer are kept, and the choices were written for this site.
  • Choices added Only the original's correct answer is known; the other choices were written for this site.

Your instructor's questions stay on the In-class page and are never mixed in here.
